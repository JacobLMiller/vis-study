# Training: Hyperbolic t-SNE

In the following tasks, you will use a **Hyperbolic t-SNE** visualization.  
This page explains how to read and interact with the visualization before you begin the study tasks.

---

## What You See

- All data points are shown at once, inside a **circular disk**. Each point is **one data item**.
- The disk shows a **hyperbolic space**: the closer a region is to the **edge** of the disk, the more it is **shrunk and squeezed together**.
- Points near the edge are therefore drawn smaller and closer together than they really are. The edge itself is infinitely far away and is never reached.
- The **center** of the disk shows its region with the **least distortion** and the most detail.
- To look at a region in detail, you **move it to the center**. The whole space then shifts, and the region you previously looked at moves towards the edge.

---

## Basic Interaction

### Panning (Moving the View)
- **Click and drag** anywhere in the disk to **pan** the view.
- Panning moves a different region of the data towards the center.

### Double-Click to Re-center
- **Double-click** on any location in the disk to **re-center** the view on that location.
- This is the fastest way to bring a region of interest into focus.

### Zooming
- Use the **mouse wheel** to zoom in and out of the disk.

### Restore Original View
- Click **Restore original view** in the top-left corner to return to the starting view at any time, for example if you are lost.

## Practice Example (Not Part of the Study)

Before starting the real tasks, you may interact with a **practice dataset**.

Use this example to:
- Pan the visualization using click-and-drag
- Double-click to re-center on different regions
- Zoom with the mouse wheel
- Observe how regions grow when they move to the center and shrink when they move to the edge
- Use **Restore original view** to return to the start

Your interactions and responses in the practice example **will not be recorded and do not count toward the study**.
