## Help

This page summarizes the controls of the two visualizations used in this study.

### Hierarchical SNE (h-SNE)

Each point is a **super-point** that summarizes a group of data points. There are 3 levels, from coarse to detailed.

- **Select points:** click and drag to draw a rectangle. You can move or resize it afterwards.
- **Clear selection:** click **Clear selection**, or click once on an empty spot in the visualization.
- **Drill down:** select points, then click **Drill down** to see them in more detail. A loading indicator is shown while the next level is computed.
- **Go back:** click **Go back** to return to the previous level.
- **Restore original view:** jumps back to the top level.
- The current level is shown above the buttons. On the most detailed level, the drill-down button reads **Finest level reached**.

### Hyperbolic t-SNE

All points are shown in a disk. Regions near the edge are shrunk; the center shows the most detail.

- **Pan:** click and drag to move a different region towards the center.
- **Re-center:** double-click a location to move it to the center.
- **Zoom:** use the mouse wheel.
- **Restore original view:** click the button in the top-left corner to return to the starting view.

### Point-shape questions

Most points are drawn as circles, but a few points are drawn with a different shape (triangle, cross, diamond or star). Find those points and select their shape. If you cannot find them, choose "I am unable to work this out".

### Problems

If a visualization stops responding, wait a few seconds; drilling down can take a while. If it still does not respond, please contact the study organizers at the email address given on the consent page.
